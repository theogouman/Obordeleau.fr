# SEO keyword research and mapping: Obordeleau.fr

Method note: this is a landscape and intent map built from live SERP research on the Les Sablettes /
La Seyne-sur-Mer holiday-rental market (Airbnb, Booking, PAP, Gites.fr, Var-Loc, HomeToGo, leboncoin).
Exact monthly volumes require a keyword tool (Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, or Ubersuggest); the owner
should validate volumes before finalizing. Priorities below are by commercial intent and observed competition.

## Positioning signals to exploit (differentiators, not just keywords)

These are what competitors under-use and our reviews prove, so they double as content hooks and long-tail terms:
- Beach on foot, about 70 m, no car needed ("sans voiture", "tout a pied", "a pied de la plage").
- Bateau-bus / shuttle boat to Toulon at the foot of the residence ("navette bateau Toulon", "embarcadere").
- 3-star classified furnished rental ("meuble de tourisme 3 etoiles") as a trust + search signal.
- Air-conditioned ("climatise"), private garden view, no facing neighbours ("sans vis-a-vis"), quiet, secured.
- Direct booking, book direct with the owner ("reservation en direct", "louer au proprietaire", no commission).
